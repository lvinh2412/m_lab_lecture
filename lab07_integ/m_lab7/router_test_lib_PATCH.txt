*** DAY LA PHAN QUAN TRONG NHAT CUA TEST-3. Doc ky. ***

Ba sequence chay SONG SONG tren ba sequencer khac nhau, trong cung
run_phase. Chung ket thuc rat khac nhau:

   +---------------------+------------------+---------------------------+
   | sequence            | ket thuc khi nao | co raise objection khong? |
   +---------------------+------------------+---------------------------+
   | yapp_012_seq        | gui xong 3 goi   | CO                        |
   | clk10_rst5_seq      | sau #300ns       | [Suy luan] xem duoi       |
   | channel_rx_resp_seq | KHONG BAO GIO    | KHONG DUOC PHEP           |
   +---------------------+------------------+---------------------------+

*** VI SAO channel_rx_resp_seq KHONG DUOC raise objection ***

No la sequence PHAN HOI. Viec cua no la lai tin hieu suspend cua kenh,
mai mai, suot ca mo phong. No khong co "diem ket thuc" tu nhien.

Neu no raise objection -> khong bao gio drop -> mo phong CHAY MAI.

=> Luat: sequence PHAN HOI (responder) khong raise objection.
         Sequence KICH THICH (stimulus) thi co.

Day la mot mo rong cua bai Section 9 ve objection. Lan dau ban thay mot
sequence CO Y khong raise.

*** clk10_rst5_seq thi sao? ***

[Suy luan] Than cua no la:
     cr.clock_period = 10; cr.reset_delay = 5; cr.run_clock = 1;
     `uvm_send(cr)
     #300ns;

Cai "#300ns" o cuoi dang chu y. Toi doan no de giu objection them 300ns
sau khi lai clock, cho reset xong han. Nhung toi CHUA doc clock_and_reset
UVC co goi set_automatic_phase_objection hay khong.

Ban tu kiem:
     grep -rn "objection\|starting_phase" ../../clock_and_reset/sv/
