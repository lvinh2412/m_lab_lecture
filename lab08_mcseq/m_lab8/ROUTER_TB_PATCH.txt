===============================================================================
[RTB-3]  connect_phase  *** HAM MOI HOAN TOAN ***
===============================================================================

Them mot ham moi, SAU build_phase:

    // UVM connect_phase
    function void connect_phase(uvm_phase phase);
      // Virtual Sequencer Connections
      mcsequencer.hbus_seqr = hbus.masters[0].sequencer;
      mcsequencer.yapp_seqr = yapp.tx_agent.sequencer;
    endfunction : connect_phase


=============================================================================
*** VI SAO PHAI O connect_phase, KHONG PHAI build_phase ***
=============================================================================

   build_phase    chay TOP-DOWN:
     router_tb.build_phase          <- ban dang o day
        |
        v
     yapp.build_phase
        |
        v
     tx_agent.build_phase           <- sequencer duoc TAO o day
        |
        v
     sequencer.build_phase

   connect_phase  chay BOTTOM-UP:
     sequencer.connect_phase
        ^
        |
     tx_agent.connect_phase
        ^
        |
     yapp.connect_phase
        ^
        |
     router_tb.connect_phase        <- ban gan o day, sequencer DA CO THAT


Dat hai dong gan trong build_phase cua router_tb:
   -> yapp.tx_agent chua ton tai  -> null pointer NGAY LUC DO
   -> va neu dat truoc dong create("yapp") thi ca yapp cung chua co


=============================================================================
*** TAI SAO masters[0] CO NGOAC VUONG ***
=============================================================================

   mcsequencer.hbus_seqr = hbus.masters[0].sequencer;   <- CO ngoac
   mcsequencer.yapp_seqr = yapp.tx_agent.sequencer;     <- KHONG

[Da xac minh] hbus_env.sv:

    hbus_master_agent masters[];        // mang DONG
    ...
    masters = new[num_masters];
    foreach(masters [i]) begin
      $sformat(inst_name, "masters[%0d]", i);
      masters[i] = hbus_master_agent::type_id::create(inst_name, this);
    end

HBUS la UVC duy nhat co SO AGENT thay doi duoc (num_masters/num_slaves).
Voi num_masters = 1 thi chi co masters[0].

Va chu y: TEN INSTANCE cung la chuoi "masters[0]" -- co ngoac vuong
TRONG TEN. Nen duong dan config cung phai viet co ngoac:
    "tb.hbus.masters[0].sequencer.run_phase"


=============================================================================
*** VI SAO CHI NOI HAI, KHONG NOI BON ***
=============================================================================

Channel UVC va Clock/Reset UVC khong duoc noi vao mcsequencer.

Vi sao? Doi chieu voi HINH SLIDE 6: thanh tim "Multichannel Sequence
Generator" chi co HAI mui ten dut net -- mot toi YAPP, mot toi HBUS.
Khong co mui ten nao toi Clock/Reset hay ba Channel UVC.

Ly do: chi hai UVC do can PHOI HOP VOI NHAU theo thu tu (ghi thanh ghi
truoc, gui goi sau). Ba Channel chi viec dap ung mai mai; Clock/Reset
chi viec chay clock mai mai -- khong ai can dieu phoi chung.

=> Chung GIU default_sequence rieng. Hai UVC kia thi XOA. [TEST-5]
